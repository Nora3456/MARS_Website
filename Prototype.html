Concept Preview

Log-In Page


Immediately upon opening the program you will see a log in page that should look like the photo attached below. To log in, enter your username and password into the “Sign in” box, then press the blue sign in button.


Light/Dark Mode Feature


When you are in the login page, this is what your navigation menu will look like:



The "☀️" | "Theme" button allows you to change between light mode (white background, black text), or dark mode (black background, white text). This demonstration will use light mode.

Dashboard


After successfully logging in, you will be brought to your dashboard. Your dashboard will look something like this:



On your dashboard you can see your most recent check-in, and a view of your vitals. You’ll notice a blue “New Check” button, here you can enter new symptoms. By pressing the button next to it (“Log Vitals”) you can enter current or recent vitals.

Navigation Sidebar


You'll notice a navigation sidebar menu to the left of your dashboard:



Let't navigate through each page.

Symptom Intake


On the symptom intake page you can log current or recent symptoms. To do this, you enter your missions day number, the severity of your symptoms on a scale from one to ten, the primary symptoms you are experiencing, and any additional notes.


Vitals


On the vitals page you can see and log your current or recent vitals. Your vitals are read by any wearable devices you are wearing. To manually input your vitals, you would use this page. You can input vitals such as your heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature.


Body Map


On the body map page you can add on to your symtom intakes by selecting where on your body that symptom is being recorded. such as the arm, head, abdomen, etc. You can select a body part by pressing the selected area with your cursor, or using the drop down menu to the right of the page.
